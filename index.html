What is the Drum Seq-uencer?

A hands-on engineering design project run by Trinity Sound. Together we’re designing a drum sequencer from scratch: the circuits, the firmware, the case and the sound.

Classic drum machines like the TR-808 turned engineering into instruments. We want to find out what it takes to build one ourselves: how the electronics work, how the sound is shaped, and how it all fits into something you can actually play.

Different departments own different parts of the build, and everything comes together in a single instrument.

  • Design
  • Prototype
  • Build

Departments

The project is split into departments, each responsible for one part of the instrument. Pick the part that interests you.

Electrical

From breadboard to board. This department takes circuit ideas from concept to a working prototype, and ultimately to a printed circuit board.

  • PCB Design
  • Waveform Modifications
  • Power Supply Design

Firmware + User Interface

The brain and the face of the instrument. Writes the code that keeps time and triggers every drum voice, and designs how it feels to use: the display, the knobs and the step buttons.

  • Sequencer & Timing Code
  • Display & Menu Design
  • Buttons, Knobs & LEDs

Enclosure

Everything you touch. Designs the case, front panel and control layout so the instrument feels good to play and is straightforward to build.

  • Case & Panel Design
  • Ergonomics
  • Fabrication & Assembly

Administration

Keeps the project running. Handles planning, budgets, sourcing parts and communication, so the other departments can focus on building.

  • Planning & Scheduling
  • Budget & Parts Sourcing
  • Outreach & Communication
